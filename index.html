Private Offline AI

An assistant that never leaves your iPhone.

The language model is inside the app. No account, no subscription, no upload. Turn on Airplane Mode and it works exactly the same.

No account No cloud required No analytics iOS 16.4+
Why this exists.

Every other AI assistant is a thin client for someone else's server. What you type goes to a data centre, gets logged, and is often used to train the next model. This one can't do that — there is no server to send anything to. The model ships in the app and runs on the phone in your hand.

What it does

Chat, offline

Draft, rewrite, summarise, explain, translate, plan. Conversations are saved on the device and nowhere else.

Read your documents

Hand it a PDF, Word or Excel file and ask about it. Text recognition runs on the phone.

Look at your photos

Point the camera at something, or pick a photo, and ask what it is. The image never leaves the device.

Talk to it

Voice input and transcription, then have it summarise what was said.

Notes and to-dos

Write a rough note, have the AI tidy it up, export it. Tasks with reminders, kept locally.

Mail

Connect Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo or iCloud and have it read, summarise and draft. Straight to your provider — nothing through us.

GameHub

Built-in games, or describe one and watch the AI write it.

Make it yours

Eight accent colours, light and dark. The whole app follows instantly.

Honest about the trade-offs

  • It is a big download. Around 800 MB, because the model comes with it. That is the price of not needing a server.
  • It is a small model. Llama 3.2 1B is chosen to run well on a phone. It is good at writing, rewriting and everyday questions, and it will sometimes get facts wrong. Check anything that matters — or connect a bigger model with your own key when you need one.
  • Nothing syncs. There is no account, so there is no sync. Your data lives on this phone and travels in your encrypted iPhone backup.
